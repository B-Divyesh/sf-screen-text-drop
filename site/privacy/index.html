Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective September 6, 2026

Your captures stay on your device

Screen Text Drop captures only when you invoke the hotkey or choose an image. OCR runs locally using bundled models. Captured pixels are held in memory only while you select a region and recognition runs and are not saved by default, uploaded, logged, or used for training.

Data stored locally

If you enter or receive a Pro license, the app stores the license token and its last verification result in local application storage. Removing the app or clearing its data removes them.

License verification

When you activate Pro, the app sends the license token to the Sociobot billing API at api.sociobot.in. Automatic checks reuse a result for up to one day. Choosing Verify checks immediately. It does not send captures or extracted text. Sociobot/Dodo acts as merchant of record for purchases and processes the information required for checkout and refunds.

No tracking

The app has no analytics, advertising identifiers, telemetry, or background recording. The website does not set analytics cookies.

Permissions

Operating systems may require Screen Recording permission. It is used only for capture you initiate. You can revoke it in system settings.
